About Silvatec Enterprises

Founded in 2015, Silvatec Enterprises has grown to become a leading technical training and services provider in the region.

Our Story & Mission

Silvatec Enterprises was established with a vision to bridge the skills gap in technical trades while providing exceptional services to the community. Over the years, we've trained thousands of skilled professionals who now contribute to various industries across the country.

Our mission is to empower individuals with practical, hands-on technical skills that lead to meaningful employment and career advancement while delivering high-quality technical services to our clients.

Mr. Silvance Obuore

Founder & Chairman

With over 30 years in the construction industry, Mr. Silvance established SilvaTech to create opportunities for young technicians.

Mr. Joseph Sylvery

Co-Founder & Dean of Studies

Joseph Sylvery is a renowned therapist with an adequate background in Counseling Psychology. He is the institution's Dean of Study and Director of studies. Mr Joseph Sylvery is a Bachelor Degree holder from Masinde Muliro University.

Mr. Evance Otieno Odima

Co-Founder

With his outstanding achievement in the field, Mr. Evance has a Bachelor Degree in Mechanical Production from The Technical University of Kenya. He is a pioneer and helped design the institute's curriculum to balance theory with practical application.
